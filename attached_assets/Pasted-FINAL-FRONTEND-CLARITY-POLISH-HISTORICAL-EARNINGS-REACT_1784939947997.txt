PART 12 — COLOR SEMANTICS

Use color according to meaning.

Directional return:

- positive return: green
- negative return: red
- zero or unavailable: neutral

Average Absolute Move:

- neutral white or cyan
- do not color it green merely because it is positive
- it measures volatility magnitude, not a bullish result

Positive Reaction Rate:

- restrained green when above 50%
- restrained red when below 50%
- neutral near 50%

Same-Direction Follow-Through and Opposite-Direction Reversal:

- use neutral/cyan presentation
- do not imply one is automatically good or bad
