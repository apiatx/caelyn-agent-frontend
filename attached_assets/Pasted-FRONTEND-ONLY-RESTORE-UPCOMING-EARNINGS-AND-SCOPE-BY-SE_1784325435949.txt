Important history:

- Primary Watchlist Upcoming Earnings previously worked and showed earnings correctly.
- The original issue was that Upcoming Earnings was not scoped to the currently selected watchlist.
- Favorites showed Primary earnings.
- Custom watchlists should show their own symbols’ earnings.

Goal:

Upcoming Earnings should show earnings for the symbols in the currently selected watchlist tab:

Primary → Primary symbols
Strong Bases → Strong Bases symbols
Favorites → favorite symbols
Any custom watchlist → that custom watchlist’s symbols
