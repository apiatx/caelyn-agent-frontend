Bucket definitions:

Market Leaders:
These are strongest fundamentals all around.
Criteria:

* usually market_cap >= 100_000_000_000
* industry anchor / beast of industry
* strong revenue base
* no major negative fundamental metrics
* positive or durable operating income / EBIT / free cash flow where available
* strong gross margin / FCF margin where available
* manageable debt metrics
* should not have obvious red-flag metrics like negative revenue growth, deeply negative margins, weak cash flow, or extreme debt

Important:
Do not put a stock in Market Leaders just because market cap is over $100B. It also needs strong overall fundamentals.

High Growth:
Strong growth stocks with mostly solid fundamentals.
Criteria:

* strong revenue growth and/or accelerating revenue
* strong positive fundamental metrics overall
* increasing financials
* positive gross margin preferred
* EPS growth or margin growth helps but is not required
* operating income / FCF can still be negative if revenue growth is very strong and other metrics are constructive
* debt can exist, but should not be an extreme red flag
* should not have multiple severe negative metrics

These are companies with real current growth and strong tailwinds, even if profitability is not fully mature yet.

Speculative Future Growth Leaders:
Forward-looking growth names with strong forecast/estimate profile but less proven current fundamentals.
Criteria:

* high revenue/EPS forecast metrics
* strong expected future growth
* may not have much current revenue yet
* may not be cash-flow positive
* may have debt
* may have weaker current profitability
* must have enough future-growth evidence to separate it from pure speculation

Use forecast fields heavily here:

* Revenue Growth Est.
* Revenue Growth Next Quarter
* Revenue Growth Next Year
* EPS Growth Est.
* EPS Growth This Quarter
* EPS Growth Next Quarter
* EPS Growth This Year
* EPS Growth Next Year

High Speculation:
Weakest fundamentals / least proven.
Criteria:

* weak current fundamentals
* little revenue support
* negative or missing profitability
* weak or missing forecast support
* high debt / negative FCF / negative operating income where available
* mostly watchlist names added for speculation rather than proven fundamentals or credible forecasts

Default bucket:
If a ticker does not qualify for the first three buckets, put it in High Speculation.

Scoring guidance:
Use all available metrics, but do not require every field to exist.

Suggested scoring dimensions:

1. Scale / anchor score

   * market cap
   * revenue size
   * industry leadership proxy

2. Current growth score

   * revenue growth Q
   * revenue growth YoY
   * EPS growth
   * revenue growth acceleration if available

3. Profitability / quality score

   * gross margin
   * FCF margin
   * free cash flow
   * operating income
   * EBIT

4. Balance sheet / risk score

   * debt / equity
   * net debt / EBITDA
   * negative cash flow / negative income penalties

5. Forecast score

   * revenue growth estimates
   * revenue growth next quarter
   * revenue growth next year
   * EPS growth estimates
   * EPS growth this/next quarter
   * EPS growth this/next year

6. Red flag score

   * negative revenue growth
   * deeply negative margins
   * negative FCF
   * negative operating income
   * extreme debt
   * missing too many fundamentals
   * very high valuation with weak growth

Bucket assignment should be deterministic:

* Market Leaders: high scale + strong quality + no severe red flags
* High Growth: high current growth + acceptable quality + no severe red flags
* Speculative Future Growth Leaders: high forecast score + weaker current quality allowed
* High Speculation: default / weak fundamentals / insufficient proof
