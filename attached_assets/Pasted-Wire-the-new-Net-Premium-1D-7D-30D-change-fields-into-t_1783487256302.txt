CORE USER GOAL
==============

The user needs to see not only current Net Premium, but whether Net Premium is becoming:

* increasingly positive
* decreasingly positive
* increasingly negative
* decreasingly negative

Examples:

+$2M → +$5M
= more positive
= bullish flow accelerating

+$8M → +$5M
= less positive
= bullish flow decelerating

-$3M → -$8M
= more negative
= bearish flow accelerating

-$8M → -$3M
= less negative
= bearish flow easing

-$2M → +$3M
= crossed positive

+$2M → -$3M
= crossed negative

The backend trend field is authoritative.

Do not infer trend from delta sign alone.
