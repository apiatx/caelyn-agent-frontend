Audit first. Do not touch backend unless required fields are missing from the already-loaded Watchlist payload. Do not add API/provider calls. Do not use an LLM.

Goal:
Refine the Watchlist `Fundamental Grouping` tab.

Current problem:
The current Fundamental Grouping tab appears to force every watchlist ticker into one of the four buckets. That is wrong.

These four columns are not exhaustive categories. They are leaderboards / signal groups.

Not every watchlist ticker needs to appear. Many tickers should appear in no column.

Scope:
Only update the Watchlist bottom `Fundamental Grouping` tab.

Do not change:

* backend
* main Tickers table
* Live News
* Stage column
* OI column/table layout
* Add ticker
* Hide Foreign
* StockDetailModal / ticker popup
* Themes tab
* Market Cap tab
* Fundamental Screener tab
* Golden Zone tab, unless it directly reuses this grouping helper

Tabs:
Keep:

* Themes
* Market Cap
* Fundamental Screener
* Fundamental Grouping

Fundamental Grouping columns:
Keep exactly 4 side-by-side columns:

1. Market Leaders
2. High Growth
3. Speculative Future Growth Leaders
4. High Speculation

Important behavior change:
Do not force every ticker into a bucket.

A ticker should appear only if it clearly qualifies for that specific category.

If a ticker does not clearly qualify for any of the four categories, omit it from Fundamental Grouping entirely.

No default bucket.

High Speculation is not the default bucket.

Data:
Use every available metric from the loaded Fundamental Screener / CSV / popup fundamentals data, including:

Core fundamentals:

* Market Cap
* Revenue
* Revenue Growth (Q)
* Revenue Growth (YoY)
* Gross Margin
* FCF Margin
* Free Cash Flow
* Operating Income
* EBIT
* P/E
* P/S
* EV/EBITDA
* EPS Growth
* Debt / Equity
* Net Debt / EBITDA
* Insider %
* Earnings Date

Forecast / estimate metrics:

* Revenue Growth Est.
* Revenue Growth Next Quarter
* Revenue Growth Next Year
* EPS Growth Est.
* EPS Growth This Quarter
* EPS Growth Next Quarter
* EPS Growth This Year
* EPS Growth Next Year

Do not use technical-only fields in the fundamental grouping score:

* 52W Low
* 52W High
* % vs MA20
* % vs MA50
* % vs MA200
* RSI
* ATR

Percentile logic:
Calculate percentiles only within the currently active watchlist ticker set.

For example:

* Primary percentiles use Primary tickers only
* Close Watch/Favorites percentiles use only Close Watch/Favorites tickers
* Future saved watchlist tabs use only their own tickers

Use top 15% thresholds for “impressive” metrics.

Top 15% means:

* metric value is at or above the 85th percentile among active watchlist rows with valid values for that metric

Do not compare against the whole market.
Do not compare against all 302 Primary tickers if the user is on Close Watch.

Create helper functions:

* parseMetricValue(row, aliases)
* getMetricPercentile(value, validValues)
* isTop15(metricName, row)
* hasSevereRedFlag(row)
* hasAnyRedFlag(row)
* getExtremeMetricTags(row)
* scoreMarketLeader(row)
* scoreHighGrowth(row)
* scoreSpecFutureGrowth(row)
* scoreHighSpeculation(row)
* assignFundamentalGroups(row)

Important:
A row can qualify for at most one displayed group. Use priority order:

1. Market Leaders
2. High Growth
3. Speculative Future Growth Leaders
4. High Speculation

But if it does not qualify for any, omit it.

Market Leaders:
These are proven anchors / beasts of industry.

Requirements:

* market_cap >= 100_000_000_000
* strong revenue base
* no negative fundamental red flags
* no red tags
* no severe red areas
* positive or durable operating income
* positive EBIT where available
* positive free cash flow where available
* positive FCF margin where available
* positive gross margin
* positive revenue growth or stable durable revenue profile
* manageable debt metrics
* not just high market cap

Disqualify from Market Leaders if any of these are true:

* revenue growth is meaningfully negative
* FCF is negative
* FCF margin is negative
* operating income is negative
* EBIT is negative
* gross margin is weak or negative
* debt/equity is extreme
* net debt/EBITDA is extreme
* multiple missing core metrics
* any negative red flag tag would be shown

Market Leaders should have zero red tags.

Allowed tags:

* $100B+ Anchor
* Huge Revenue Base
* Strong Gross Mgn
* Strong FCF Mgn
* Positive EBIT
* Low Debt
* Insider Heavy
* Cash Machine

High Growth:
These are current growth leaders with impressive active growth metrics.

Requirements:

* must have at least one current growth metric in the top 15% of the active watchlist:

  * Revenue Growth (Q)
  * Revenue Growth (YoY)
  * EPS Growth
  * Gross Margin
  * FCF Margin
* OR an extremely strong combination of current growth metrics just below top 15%
* strong positive fundamental profile overall
* no severe red flags
* no multiple major red flags
* operating income / FCF can be negative if revenue growth is elite and other quality metrics are constructive
* debt can exist, but must not be an extreme red flag

Do not include a stock in High Growth just because it has one okay positive metric. It needs standout growth.

Use top 15% as the primary gate.

Good tags:

* Top 15% Rev Growth
* Huge Rev Growth
* Top 15% EPS Growth
* Top 15% Gross Mgn
* Top 15% FCF Mgn
* Strong Growth
* Strong Quality

Disqualify or downgrade if:

* revenue growth is negative
* EPS growth is deeply negative
* gross margin is weak
* FCF margin is deeply negative
* operating income and FCF are both bad
* debt is extreme
* forecast metrics are weak and current fundamentals are also weak

Speculative Future Growth Leaders:
These are forward-looking leaders with impressive forecasts or growth investment profiles.

Requirements:
Must have at least one of these:

A. Forecast strength:

* Revenue Growth Est. in top 15%
* Revenue Growth Next Quarter in top 15%
* Revenue Growth Next Year in top 15%
* EPS Growth Est. in top 15%
* EPS Growth This Quarter in top 15%
* EPS Growth Next Quarter in top 15%
* EPS Growth This Year in top 15%
* EPS Growth Next Year in top 15%

OR

B. Current revenue ramp with investment burden:

* huge revenue growth / revenue acceleration
* but also signs of investment burden, such as:

  * increasing debt
  * weak/negative FCF
  * weak/negative operating income
  * EPS decline
  * margin pressure

These are companies that may be setting up for big future growth/profitability but are still expensive, leveraged, or not fully proven.

Do not include names that have weak current fundamentals and weak forecasts. Those belong in High Speculation only if they clearly qualify as avoids.

Good tags:

* Top 15% Forecasts
* Top 15% Rev Forecast
* Top 15% EPS Forecast
* Huge Rev Ramp
* Growth Investment
* Future Leader
* Forecast-Led

Risk tags are allowed here:

* Negative FCF
* Debt Funding Growth
* Margin Pressure
* EPS Pressure
* Not Profitable Yet

High Speculation:
These are avoid / weak-fundamental names.

Column subtitle should include:

* Avoid

Rename subtitle:
High Speculation — Avoid / Weak Fundamentals

Requirements:
Only include tickers that have weak fundamentals and do not have impressive future forecasts.

Must have several negative indicators, such as:

* revenue decline
* weak or missing revenue
* negative FCF
* negative FCF margin
* negative operating income
* negative EBIT
* weak gross margin
* high debt
* negative EPS growth
* weak or missing forecasts
* no top 15% future forecast metric
* no top 15% current growth metric

Important:
Do not use High Speculation as a default catch-all.

If a ticker simply lacks enough data but does not clearly show weak fundamentals, omit it instead of putting it in High Speculation.

High Speculation rows should display an `AVOID` badge.

Bad tags:

* AVOID
* Rev Decline
* Negative FCF
* Neg Op Income
* Weak Gross Mgn
* High Debt
* Weak Forecasts
* No Proof
* Missing Fundamentals
