Core idea:

Most investing/trading platforms offer data, screeners, swing setups, alerts, watchlists, and AI summaries.

The problem is that users still have to manually connect everything:

* technical setup
* trend strength
* theme leadership
* options flow
* fundamentals
* catalysts
* social sentiment
* portfolio exposure
* personal rules

Most people do not have the time, process, or discipline to cross-check all of that well enough to make high-conviction decisions.

What they are missing is CONFLUENCE.

Caelyn's differentiator is that it helps investors see when multiple independent signals are lining up around the same ticker, theme, setup, or portfolio decision.
