Goal:
Add a new bottom tab on the Watchlist page called:

High Conviction Investment Zone

This tab should identify the strongest watchlist names sitting at the intersection of:

1. elite current or future fundamental growth
2. constructive early technical stage

* Fundamental Screener

Dependency:
This tab should reuse the same deterministic helper logic created for `Fundamental Grouping`.

* getFundamentalMetrics(row)

Eligibility:
A ticker qualifies for `High Conviction Investment Zone` only if all of these are true:

1. It qualifies as either:

   * High Growth
   * Speculative Future Growth Leaders

Use the same logic/bucket assignment from the `Fundamental Grouping` tab.

2. It has elite growth characteristics.

For High Growth:
Must have at least one of these current growth/quality metrics in the top 15% of the active watchlist:

* Revenue Growth (Q)
* Revenue Growth (YoY)
* EPS Growth
* Gross Margin
* FCF Margin

Primary emphasis:

* highest revenue growth
* highest EPS growth

For Speculative Future Growth Leaders:
Must have at least one of these future/forecast metrics in the top 15% of the active watchlist:

* Revenue Growth Est.
* Revenue Growth Next Quarter
* Revenue Growth Next Year
* EPS Growth Est.
* EPS Growth This Quarter
* EPS Growth Next Quarter
* EPS Growth This Year
* EPS Growth Next Year
* margin increase forecast fields, if available

Primary emphasis:

* highest future revenue growth forecasts
* highest future EPS growth forecasts
* highest future margin improvement forecasts, if available

3. It is in one of these constructive early stages:
   Use:
   row.stage_analysis?.label || row.stage2_breakout?.label

Allowed stage labels:

* S1 Base
* S1-2 Watch
* S2 Breakout

Do not include:

* S2-S3 Advance
* S3 Momentum
* S3-S4 Top
* S4 Decline

Reason:
This tab is for names that are fundamentally compelling and still early enough technically to be actionable.

Percentile logic:
Calculate top 15% thresholds only within the currently active saved watchlist.

Examples:

* Primary uses Primary tickers only
* Close Watch/Favorites uses only Close Watch/Favorites tickers
* future saved watchlist tabs use only their own tickers

Do not calculate percentiles against all Primary tickers when another watchlist tab is active.
