2) About modal content

Use these definitions:

Ticker:
The stock or ETF symbol.

Type:
Whether the symbol is a stock or ETF.

Price:
Current price of the underlying stock or ETF.

Score:
Main signal score from our options-flow model. Higher means stronger unusual activity.

Heat:
Extra momentum/intensity score using premium, volume vs open interest, urgency, and unusual contract behavior.

Signal:
The type of setup detected, such as unusual flow or asymmetric risk/reward.

Bias:
Whether the options activity leans bullish, bearish, mixed, or unclear.

Move %:
Recent move in the underlying stock or ETF.

Premium:
Estimated dollar value of the options activity. Bigger premium usually means larger money is involved.

Prem Δ%:
Change in options premium versus the previous snapshot. Rising premium can mean interest is accelerating.

Vol:
Options contract volume.

OI:
Open interest. This is the number of existing open contracts.

OI Δ%:
Change in open interest versus the previous snapshot. Rising OI can suggest new positioning.

V/OI:
Volume divided by open interest. High V/OI can indicate unusual fresh activity.

Call %:
Percent of flow leaning toward calls. Calls often suggest bullish positioning.

Put %:
Percent of flow leaning toward puts. Puts often suggest bearish or hedging activity.

DTE:
Days to expiration. Lower DTE means the trade is more urgent/speculative.

Strike:
The option contract’s strike price.

OTM %:
How far the contract is out-of-the-money. Far OTM contracts are higher risk, higher reward.

Unusual:
Flags contracts that are meaningfully out-of-the-money with strong premium and unusual volume/open-interest behavior.
