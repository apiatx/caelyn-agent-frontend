PART 3 — PRIMARY FRONTEND CONTRACT
==================================================

For each row, use:

row.confluence_v42

Primary score:

confluence_v42.score.total
confluence_v42.score.core
confluence_v42.score.bonus
confluence_v42.score.core_max
confluence_v42.score.bonus_max
confluence_v42.score.total_max
confluence_v42.score.display_mode
confluence_v42.score.percent_of_total_max

Display rule:

Main score should emphasize:

Core / 100
Bonus +X / 25

Do NOT make /125 the main visual denominator.

Good display examples:

Score: 72.6
Core: 69.5 / 100
Bonus: +3.1 / 25

or:

72.6 total
Core 69.5/100 · Bonus +3.1

The total max is 125, but do not visually make 72.6/125 look like a weak 58% score.

PART 8 — RISK FLAGS VS DATA STATUS FLAGS
==================================================

True risk flags:

confluence_v42.risk.risk_flags

These can be shown as risk warnings.

Examples:
EXTREME_EXTENSION
CHASE_EXTENSION
MAJOR_LOWER_LOW_CONFIRMED
LOWER_LOW_CONFIRMED
RISK_CONFLICT
AVOID_SIGNAL
NO_CLEAR_ENTRY

Data / coverage / pending flags:

confluence_v42.metadata.data_status_flags

These are NOT bearish by default.

Examples:
FIB_PENDING
OPTIONS_UNAVAILABLE
CATALYST_UNAVAILABLE
WHALE_INSIDER_NOT_WIRED

Display data_status_flags separately as neutral coverage badges, not red risk warnings.

Important:

FIB_PENDING means waiting for 10Y backfill / Fib-Wave population.
It does not mean the stock is technically bad.
