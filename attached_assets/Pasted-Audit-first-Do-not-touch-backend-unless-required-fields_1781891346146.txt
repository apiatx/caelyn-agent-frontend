Audit first. Do not touch backend unless required fields are missing from the already-loaded Watchlist payload. Do not add API/provider calls. Do not use an LLM.

Goal:
Add a new bottom tab on the Watchlist page called:

Golden Zone

This tab should be the first tab in the bottom section and should be selected by default when the user visits the Watchlist page.

Golden Zone should show the highest-quality setups that appear strong from both:

1. High Conviction Investment Zone
2. High Conviction Trade Zone

These are A+ setups:

* strong current/future fundamental growth
* constructive early technical stage
* strong trading signal confluence
* good candidate for either a high-conviction trade or a long-term entry

Tabs:
Reorder bottom tabs so `Golden Zone` is first.

Expected order:

1. Golden Zone
2. Themes
3. Market Cap
4. Fundamental Screener
5. Fundamental Grouping
6. High Conviction Investment Zone
7. High Conviction Trade Zone

Default:

* `Golden Zone` should be the default selected bottom tab on Watchlist page load.
* If localStorage currently remembers another bottom tab, either clear/update that behavior or only use localStorage after the user manually changes tabs.
* The user should land on Golden Zone by default.

Scope:
Only update Watchlist bottom-section tabs.

Do not change:

* backend
* main Tickers table
* Live News
* Stage column
* OI column/table layout
* Add ticker
* Hide Foreign
* StockDetailModal / ticker popup
* existing High Conviction Investment Zone logic except making its derived rows reusable
* existing High Conviction Trade Zone logic except making its derived rows reusable

Architecture:
Do not create a third independent scoring formula from scratch.

Reuse the existing derived logic from:

* High Conviction Investment Zone
* High Conviction Trade Zone

If those tabs currently compute rows inside render branches, extract/reuse helpers so Golden Zone can consume the same results.

Suggested shared helpers:

* getHighConvictionInvestmentRows(rows)
* getHighConvictionTradeRows(rows)
* getGoldenZoneRows(rows)

Golden Zone eligibility:
A ticker qualifies for Golden Zone if:

1. It qualifies for High Conviction Investment Zone
   AND
2. It qualifies for High Conviction Trade Zone

Preferred strict intersection:

* Use tickers that appear in both derived row sets.

Fallback if strict intersection is too empty:

* Include names that strongly qualify for one side and are near-qualifying on the other side.
* But only if they still have:

  * allowed early stage
  * at least one elite investment/fundamental growth signal
  * at least one loud trade signal

Do not loosen rules just to fill the tab with mediocre names.

Allowed stages:
Golden Zone must only include:

* S1 Base
* S1-2 Watch
* S2 Breakout

Do not include:

* S2-S3 Advance
* S3 Momentum
* S3-S4 Top
* S4 Decline

Investment-side signals:
Golden Zone names should have strong current or future fundamental growth such as:

* high revenue growth
* high EPS growth
* high revenue growth forecast
* high EPS forecast
* high future margin improvement forecast, if available
* High Growth classification
* Speculative Future Growth classification with strong forecast/ramp evidence

Trade-side signals:
Golden Zone names should also have a strong trade setup mix:

* high VolX
* high Vol / Market Cap
* strong options score/signal
* constructive early stage
* weighted trade confluence score from High Conviction Trade Zone

Scoring:
Create a blended `goldenZoneScore`.

Suggested weights:

* Investment score: 50%
* Trade score: 40%
* Stage quality: 10%

If Investment Zone and Trade Zone already produce scores, reuse them:

* investmentConvictionScore
* tradeConvictionScore

If names are strict intersection:
goldenZoneScore = investmentScore * 0.5 + tradeScore * 0.4 + stageScore * 0.1

Stage score:

* S2 Breakout = 100
* S1-2 Watch = 85
* S1 Base = 70

Sorting:
Sort strongest Golden Zone setups first:

1. goldenZoneScore descending
2. S2 Breakout first
3. tradeConvictionScore descending
4. investmentConvictionScore descending
5. VolX descending
6. options score descending
