Refine the `High Conviction Trade Zone` logic. Do not touch backend. Do not add API/provider calls. Do not use an LLM.

Current issue:
The tab is returning 0 qualifying rows because the qualification gate is too strict.

Current gate appears to require:

* allowed stage
* at least 2 of 3 signal groups strong
* conviction score >= 60

That is too binary.

Goal:
Keep the tab honest, but make it weighted. A ticker does not need to check every signal box. It needs the best mix of trade signals relative to the active watchlist.

Important:
Do not label weak setups as high conviction.
But also do not require every ticker to have options signal + VolX + Vol/MC all firing at once.

Core rule:
Allowed stage is mandatory.
Signal confluence should be weighted and relative, not perfect-checklist based.

Allowed stages:
Use:
row.stage_analysis?.label || row.stage2_breakout?.label

Only these stages qualify:

* S1 Base
* S1-2 Watch
* S2 Breakout

Do not include:

* S2-S3 Advance
* S3 Momentum
* S3-S4 Top
* S4 Decline

Signal fields:
Use already-loaded fields only:

* options_score / opt_score / flow_score
* options_signal / opt_signal
* relative_volume / rel_vol / volx
* vol_mc_pct
* vol_mc_ratio
* volume / market_cap fallback
* price
* change percent

Do not fetch anything.

Revised scoring:
Create a weighted `tradeConvictionScore` where missing/quiet signals reduce score but do not automatically disqualify.

Suggested weights:

* VolX strength: 35%
* Vol / Market Cap strength: 25%
* Options score / options signal strength: 25%
* Stage quality: 15%

Reason:
VolX and Vol/MC often catch real-time setups even when options data is weak/missing. Options should boost conviction, not act as a hard requirement for every candidate.

Stage score:

* S2 Breakout = 100
* S1-2 Watch = 85
* S1 Base = 70

VolX strength:
Use active-watchlist percentiles plus absolute thresholds.

Score high if:

* VolX >= 5.0, or top 5% of active watchlist
* VolX >= 2.0, or top 15% of active watchlist
* VolX is meaningfully above normal

Vol / MC strength:
Use active-watchlist percentile.
Score high if:

* top 5% = elite
* top 15% = strong
* top 25% = notable

Options strength:
Use numeric options score if available.

* > = 85 = elite
* > = 70 = strong
* > = 50 = useful
  > If numeric score is missing, use options_signal / options activity fields if available.
  > If options data is missing, do not auto-disqualify. Just give 0 for the options component.

Qualification rules:
A ticker qualifies if:

1. It is in an allowed stage, AND

2. It has at least one loud signal:

   * VolX top 15% or VolX >= 2.0
   * OR Vol/MC top 15%
   * OR options score >= 50
   * OR options signal is clearly positive/unusual

AND

3. It passes either:

   * tradeConvictionScore >= 55
   * OR it is in the top 10 ranked early-stage trade candidates by tradeConvictionScore, with score >= 45

This keeps the tab selective but prevents it from going empty when the best setups are strong in only one or two dimensions.

Important:
Do not require at least 2 of 3 signal groups to be strong.
Instead:

* one elite signal + good stage can qualify
* two solid signals + good stage can qualify
* three decent signals can qualify
* stage alone cannot qualify
* high IV alone cannot qualify
* options data missing does not automatically fail the row

Examples that should qualify:

* S2 Breakout + elite VolX + mediocre options + average Vol/MC
* S1-2 Watch + top 5% Vol/MC + useful options score
* S1 Base + VolX top 5% + high Vol/MC
* S2 Breakout + options score > 70 + VolX above normal

Examples that should not qualify:

* S3 Momentum with great options
* S4 Decline with high VolX
* S1 Base with no signal strength
* high IV only
* high options activity but no VolX, no Vol/MC, weak stage context
* missing all trade signal fields

Tags:
Update tags to reflect the weighted logic.

Sorting:
Sort by:

1. tradeConvictionScore descending
2. stage priority:

   * S2 Breakout
   * S1-2 Watch
   * S1 Base
3. VolX percentile descending
4. Vol/MC percentile descending
5. options score descending

Empty state:
Keep the empty state if truly nothing qualifies.
But with the revised weighted logic, do not return empty just because no ticker has 2 of 3 signal groups strong.

Remove noisy console logs after validation.
