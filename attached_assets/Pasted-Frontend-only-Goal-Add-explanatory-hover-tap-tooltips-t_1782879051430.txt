Add header explanations for:

MA Stack:
Shows whether short/intermediate/long moving averages are aligned bullishly, mixed, or bearishly. Bull usually means constructive trend; Bear usually means weak or broken trend.

% vs 50D:
Price distance from the 50-day moving average. Near/above can be healthy; very far above can mean extended; below can signal weakness.

% vs 200D:
Price distance from the 200-day moving average. Shows long-term trend context. Below 200D is usually weaker.

Extension Risk:
Flags whether price is healthy, extended, overheated, in a pullback zone, or broken. Helps avoid chasing names too far above key moving averages.

52W Pos:
Where price sits in its 52-week range from 0–100%. Higher means closer to yearly highs/leadership; very low means damaged or early recovery.

% From 52W High:
How far below the 52-week high price is. Near 0 means near highs/breakout area; deeply negative means more overhead supply or damage.

Entry Zone:
Interpreted entry timing label. Shows whether the stock is near a 20D/50D pullback, breakout watch, fresh breakout, extended, overheated, broken, or neutral.

Breakout Signal:
Detects coiling, near-trigger, fresh breakout, confirmed breakout, extended breakout, failed breakout, or no setup. Helps separate actionable breakouts from chase/failed setups.

Accum/Dist:
Uses recent price/volume behavior to estimate accumulation versus distribution. Accumulation suggests demand; distribution suggests selling pressure; dry-up can indicate a base or squeeze.

Squeeze:
Shows volatility compression or expansion. Tight/coiling can indicate setup energy; volatile means wider risk; expansion means the move may already be underway.

ATR %:
Average true range as a percent of price. Helps size risk; higher ATR means the stock is more volatile.

Momentum Trend:
Uses recent price momentum / rate-of-change trend. Positive or accelerating supports the trend; cooling, diverging, or negative warns momentum is fading.

Technical State:
Summary chart condition: Coiling, Pullback Entry, Breakout Trigger, Trend Advance, Extended, Overheated, Distribution, Broken, or Neutral. This is not a buy/sell rating; it is chart timing context.
