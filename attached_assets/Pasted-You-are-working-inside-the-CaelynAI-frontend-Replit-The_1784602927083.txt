## Part 9 — Direction-aware colors

Do not use one universal “positive number = green” formatter for every metric.

Use these semantics:

### Positive generally constructive

* Incremental Operating Margin
* Forward Revenue Growth
* Revenue Estimate Revision 90D

### Diluted Shares Growth

This is reversed:

* Negative growth/buybacks → green
* Positive growth/dilution → red
* Near zero → neutral

### Cash Runway

* Self-Funding → green
* Adequate or 24+ months → green/teal
* Caution or 12–24 months → amber
* Critical or under 12 months → red
* N/M → muted gray

### Altman risk

* Safe → green
* Grey → amber
* Distress → red
* N/M → muted gray

### Valuation multiples

Keep valuation multiples neutral.

Do not automatically color a low P/E green or high P/E red. Valuation context varies by company and growth rate.

## Part 11 — Tooltips

Add concise header tooltips using the existing tooltip components.

Required explanations:

* Net Cash/Debt: Cash and short-term investments minus total debt
* Cash Runway: Cash divided by annualized cash burn when FCF is negative
* Current Ratio: Current assets divided by current liabilities
* Interest Coverage: EBIT divided by interest expense
* Altman Z: Balance-sheet distress indicator; not meaningful for certain financial structures
* Piotroski: Nine-point financial-strength score
* ROIC: Return generated on invested capital
* FCF Conversion: TTM free cash flow divided by materially positive TTM net income
* Diluted Shares Growth: Current four-quarter average diluted shares versus prior four-quarter average
* SBC/Revenue: Stock-based compensation as a percentage of revenue
* Revenue Acceleration: Latest quarterly YoY growth minus prior quarterly YoY growth
* Gross Margin Change: Current TTM gross margin minus prior-year TTM gross margin
* Incremental Operating Margin: Change in TTM operating income divided by positive change in TTM revenue
* Forward Revenue Growth: FY1 consensus revenue versus latest completed actual fiscal-year revenue
* Revision 90D: Current FY1 consensus versus the stored observation nearest 90 days ago
* Forward P/E: Current price divided by FY1 annual consensus EPS when available
* Forward P/S: Current market cap divided by FY1 consensus revenue
* Forward EV/Sales: Enterprise value divided by FY1 consensus revenue
* Forward EV/EBITDA: Enterprise value divided by positive FY1 consensus EBITDA
* P/FCF: Market capitalization divided by positive TTM free cash flow

Keep tooltips short enough to scan.
