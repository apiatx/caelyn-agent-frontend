Goal:
Clean up Screener Hub columns and options display now that backend options fields are standardized.

Backend now provides:
- options_oi

Important backend semantics:
- options_oi = open interest contracts, not dollars
- options_oi_change_pct = percent change in OI versus previous cached snapshot
- options_oi_change_pct is percentage points: 12.5 = +12.5%
- options_oi_change_status explains why pct is missing, e.g. prior_zero or no_prior_snapshot
