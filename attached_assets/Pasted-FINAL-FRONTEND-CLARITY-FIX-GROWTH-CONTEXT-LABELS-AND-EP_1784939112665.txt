CURRENT PROBLEM

Growth Context currently displays values such as:

Revenue QoQ  -10.8%
Revenue YoY  +27.5%
EPS QoQ      Profit ↓
EPS YoY      Profit ↑

The EPS wording is ambiguous. “Profit ↑” and “Profit ↓” do not explain:

- which periods are being compared
- the EPS values involved
- whether EPS increased or decreased
- why a percentage is not shown

OBJECTIVE

Make every comparison self-explanatory while preserving the existing compact professional layout.

PART 1 — AUDIT THE EXISTING FIELDS

Inspect the current Growth Context implementation and identify the exact fields used for:

- latest-quarter revenue
- previous-quarter revenue
- same-quarter-prior-year revenue
- latest-quarter EPS
- previous-quarter EPS
- same-quarter-prior-year EPS
- existing Revenue QoQ and Revenue YoY percentages
- existing EPS semantic state

Do not guess field names.

Confirm the existing calculations before changing presentation.

Do not change their source or calculation in this task unless the frontend is clearly displaying the wrong comparison.

PART 2 — REVENUE LABELS

Replace abbreviated labels with clearer labels:

Revenue vs Previous Quarter
Revenue vs Year Ago

Continue showing the percentage:

Revenue vs Previous Quarter
−10.8%

Revenue vs Year Ago
+27.5%

Where space permits, add muted comparison values beneath:

$10.0M → $8.9M

Use:

- previous fiscal quarter for the first comparison
- matching fiscal quarter one year earlier for the second comparison

These are point-to-point comparisons, not historical averages.

PART 3 — EPS POSITIVE-TO-POSITIVE COMPARISONS

When current EPS and comparison EPS are both positive and the denominator is valid, show the actual percentage change rather than “Profit ↑” or “Profit ↓”.

Example:

EPS vs Previous Quarter
−12.4%
$0.42 → $0.37

EPS vs Year Ago
+31.8%
$0.28 → $0.37

Use the existing percentage field if available.

Otherwise calculate only from the correctly matched EPS values:

((current EPS / comparison EPS) - 1) × 100

Do not calculate when the comparison EPS is zero, negative, missing, or too close to zero for a meaningful percentage.

PART 4 — EPS SIGN-CHANGE STATES

Use explicit semantic descriptions when percentage growth would be misleading.

Previous negative, current positive:

Turned Profitable
−$0.06 → +$0.08

Previous positive, current negative:

Turned to a Loss
+$0.12 → −$0.04

Both negative and current is less negative:

Loss Narrowed
−$0.18 → −$0.09

Both negative and current is more negative:

Loss Widened
−$0.07 → −$0.15

Both zero or unavailable:

No Meaningful Comparison
or
—

Do not display generic:

Profit ↑
Profit ↓
Loss ↑
Loss ↓

PART 5 — CLEAR PERIOD NAMES

Use:

- Revenue vs Previous Quarter
- Revenue vs Year Ago
- EPS vs Previous Quarter
- EPS vs Year Ago

Avoid unexplained QoQ and YoY abbreviations in the primary labels.

A small muted “QoQ” or “YoY” badge may remain only if useful.

PART 6 — VISUAL TREATMENT

Preserve the current Growth Context metric-tile design.

Each tile should contain:

1. clear comparison label
2. primary result or semantic state
3. muted from-value → to-value line where data exists

Examples:

−12.4%
$0.42 → $0.37

EPS VS YEAR AGO
TURNED PROFITABLE
−$0.06 → +$0.08

Use restrained colors:

- improvement: green
- deterioration: red
- neutral/unavailable: gray
- do not rely on arrows alone

PART 7 — NULL AND EDGE CASES

Do not show:

- 0%
- Profit
- Loss
- Up
- Down

when the underlying values are missing.

Do not divide by zero.

Do not use a normal percentage when either EPS value is negative.

Do not classify a smaller negative EPS as worsening; it is a narrowing loss.
