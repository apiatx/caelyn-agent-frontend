When a user clicks an open option position in the Portfolio Dashboard Open Options section, open a TradingView-style popup for the underlying stock and show the option position details, live Tradier mark/valuation, portfolio-scoped options signal, and portfolio risk score.

Important:
This popup is only for Portfolio Dashboard open options positions.
Do not change the global Options Flow page.
Do not call Tradier directly from frontend.
Do not calculate options signal/risk in frontend.
Do not hardcode BWEN, FCEL, GSIT, SKYT, VIVO, or any ticker.
Do not break the existing Core/Open Positions TradingView popup.
Do not put open options back into Trade Journal.
