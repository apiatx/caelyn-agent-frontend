Context from backend audit:
Signal Lab and Trade Radar are different engines:
- Signal Lab uses /api/hyperliquid/screener/hero.
- Signal Lab is more structure/regime/quality gated, with a higher volume floor.
- Signal Lab is better for cleaner 4h–1d swing ideas.
- Trade Radar uses /api/hyperliquid/screener/trade-radar.
- Trade Radar uses a wider universe and 6-component tactical composite: momentum, positioning, funding, flow, microstructure, risk.
- Trade Radar is better for faster 15m–4h tactical pressure and early signals.

Goal:
Make the UI explain this distinction so users are not confused when the tabs show different tickers.
