Important backend behavior:
After an admin edit, /api/themes/admin/theme-basket/{theme_id} reflects the edit immediately.
/api/themes/relative-strength may temporarily serve stale-good 60-theme LKG with admin_refresh_pending: true until full recompute finishes.

Therefore:
The expanded ThemeBasketPanel should use the admin basket endpoint as the immediate source of truth after mutations. Do not rely only on /api/themes/relative-strength for instant panel updates.
