Goal:
Add a new bottom tab on the Watchlist page called:

Growth Momentum

This tab should show tickers with strong growth fundamentals and momentum confirmation.

Important distinction:
This is different from Golden Zone.

* Golden Zone = early entry / breakout setup, usually S1 Base, S1-2 Watch, or S2 Breakout
* Growth Momentum = stocks already in motion, specifically S2 Breakout, S2-S3 Advance, or S3 Momentum

This tab should identify high-quality or high-growth names that are already moving and still have strong revenue/EPS/fundamental momentum plus trading confirmation.

Growth Momentum

Eligibility:
A ticker qualifies for Growth Momentum only if all of these are true:

1. It belongs to one of these fundamental groups:

* Market Leaders
* High Growth
* Speculative Future Growth Leaders

Use the same deterministic grouping helper already used by `Fundamental Grouping`.

Do not include:

* High Speculation
* Avoid / weak fundamental names

2. It is in one of these momentum stages:
   Use:
   row.stage_analysis?.label || row.stage2_breakout?.label

Allowed stages:

* S2 Breakout
* S2-S3 Advance
* S3 Momentum

Do not include:

* S1 Base
* S1-2 Watch
* S3-S4 Top
* S4 Decline

3. It has strong growth/fundamental momentum.

Require a strong mix of the following:

* big revenue
* increasing revenue
* positive EPS where available
* EPS growth
* revenue growth
* gross margin strength
* FCF margin strength
* future revenue growth forecasts
* future EPS growth forecasts

Primary emphasis:

* revenue growth
* EPS growth
* future revenue growth
* future EPS growth
* margin expansion if available

4. It has options score >= 25.

Use existing loaded options fields only.

Possible aliases:

* options_score
* option_score
* optionsScore
* opt_score
* flow_score
* options_activity_score
* unusual_options_score

If no options score exists, ticker does not qualify.

5. It has high VolX.

Use existing loaded VolX / relative volume fields:

* relative_volume
* rel_vol
* volx
* vol_x

High VolX should mean either:

* VolX >= 1.5
  OR
* top 25% VolX among the active watchlist

Stronger tags:

* VolX >= 2.0 = High VolX
* VolX >= 5.0 = Elite VolX
* top 15% active watchlist = Rel Vol Leader
* top 5% active watchlist = Elite Rel Vol

This tab should not require extreme VolX if the stock has very strong growth and strong stage momentum, but it should not include quiet names with no volume signal.

Do not force every ticker into this tab.
Only show tickers that clearly qualify.

Scoring:
Create a deterministic `growthMomentumScore`.

Suggested weights:

* Growth/fundamental momentum: 40%
* Stage momentum: 25%
* VolX strength: 20%
* Options score: 15%

Stage score:

* S2 Breakout = 90
* S2-S3 Advance = 100
* S3 Momentum = 85

Reason:
S2-S3 Advance is usually the cleanest growth momentum zone.
S2 Breakout is earlier and powerful.
S3 Momentum is strong but more mature/extended, so still useful but slightly lower.

Growth gates:
A ticker should generally have at least one of:

* Revenue Growth (Q) in top 25% of active watchlist
* Revenue Growth (YoY) in top 25%
* EPS Growth in top 25%
* Revenue Growth forecast in top 25%
* EPS Growth forecast in top 25%
* very large revenue base plus positive EPS and positive growth
* Market Leader with positive EPS, positive revenue trend, and high VolX

For High Growth and Speculative Future Growth Leaders, prefer top 15% current or forecast growth metrics when available.

Market Leaders can qualify with less explosive growth if they have:

* big revenue
* positive EPS
* positive EPS growth
* positive revenue growth
* strong VolX
* allowed momentum stage

Speculative Future Growth Leaders can qualify if:

* future revenue/EPS forecasts are very strong
* current revenue is ramping
* VolX is high
* options score >= 25
* stage is S2 Breakout, S2-S3 Advance, or S3 Momentum

Disqualify:

* High Speculation / Avoid names
* negative or collapsing revenue without strong future forecasts
* negative EPS with weak forecasts
* weak/missing growth metrics
* options score below 25
* quiet volume / low VolX
* S3-S4 Top
* S4 Decline
